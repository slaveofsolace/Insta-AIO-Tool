# 3.1.5 compatibility

**Check mutuals** reads Followers and Following through one paginated traversal each. It does not depend on all rows being mounted in an Instagram dialog. Both traversals and a final profile-total check finish before the pair is saved.

The optional dialog scanner starts at the top and saves overlapping windows before scrolling further. It retains accounts when rows are recycled and restarts from the top when the scroller is replaced. Completion requires a known exact total matching the unique captured accounts. A quiet list, spinner disappearance, or unchanged scroll height does not establish completeness.

If either list is incomplete, the toolbox withholds the comparison and its downloads. Partial rows can still be exported separately under Advanced. Missing accounts are not classified as non-mutuals. Instagram may withhold entries or return no continuation cursor; the toolbox cannot force the server to expose them. Matching counts also cannot prove an atomic snapshot if memberships change during a scan.

Capture schema 6 preserves saved rows, settings, pairings, and ledgers. Older dialog captures need a fresh scan before they can be considered complete; previously verified authenticated captures remain usable. No new permissions or Instagram endpoints are added.
