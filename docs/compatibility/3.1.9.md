# 3.1.9 compatibility

Open a profile and choose **Check Followers + Following**. Leave the profile open until the check finishes. Neither list needs to be opened or scrolled.

The checker keeps one cursor traversal per list. Exact displayed totals avoid unnecessary profile-count requests. Missing, limited, unfinished, or changing lists cannot produce an inferred non-mutual comparison. Raw partial captures remain under **Capture lists and export**.

On a rate limit, the checker waits for Retry-After when supplied. Otherwise, waits start at five and ten minutes. The countdown distinguishes an Instagram-provided wait from automatic backoff. Stop cancels a pending retry. See [cooldowns](../MUTUAL_CHECKER_COOLDOWNS.md) for the deadline and same-tab limits.

Login, challenge, account restriction, or profile changes stop the check. No alternative endpoint or identity is tried to bypass a restriction. Cooldowns do not guarantee complete server responses or a successful authenticated comparison.

Storage schemas, pairing, exports, queues, and ledgers are unchanged. No permissions or endpoints are added. DM Unsend still stops when the open conversation changes.
