# 3.1.11 acceptance

This patch makes captured mutual comparisons available in the panel and downloads even when a list is partial. Uncertain differences use “Not found” labels rather than definite relationship claims. The pagination driver, cooldowns, storage schemas, and action engines are unchanged.

## Checks

- Assembly, 413/413 tests, generated-userscript parity, and extension/web archive checks passed locally.
- Production extension and userscript fixtures show partial comparisons, uncertainty labels, and working downloads. Complete-list action guards remain covered, including one-sided and older unverified captures.
- Chrome pairing, 11/11 PWA screenshot states, and the dependency audit passed.
- The two partial-capture overlay deltas were reviewed before generating the versioned baseline. They replace hidden comparisons with captured results and downloads. The other 43 overlay states stayed within the existing tolerance; thresholds were not changed. Historical baselines are retained.
- A non-updating check passed all 45 overlay states. The reviewed baseline manifest SHA-256 is `de43ef067b714099148bb0dbc7c9bf6a3d25aeb75169c34bfad8a5308a3cb38a`.
- The userscript layout fixture covers light/dark, short laptop, 480px, narrow custom panel, mobile portrait/landscape, and true 200% zoom. Keyboard and Chromium accessibility-tree assertions passed.
- Release promotion requires the exact main revision's complete CI matrix, visual checks, Windows/macOS package lifecycle checks, archive checks, and checksums.

## Limits

Partial results describe captured accounts only. They do not identify missing accounts or establish whether those accounts are deactivated, moderated, or omitted by Instagram. Comparison-based action sources still require complete verified lists.

No live Instagram requests or account mutations are part of this patch's checks. Automated fixtures do not prove full-list availability, Firefox live behavior, or human screen-reader acceptance. Windows is unsigned; macOS packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
